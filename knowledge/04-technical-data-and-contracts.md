# Technical data and confidential contracts

> Demo knowledge base: proposed rules for the hackathon, to be confirmed by Compliance. Not official Forto policy.

## Technical specifications

Specification sheets with performance values, such as operating frequencies, positioning accuracy or temperature ranges, can describe controlled technology. Until a compliance manager has validated the case, they stay on-prem and are not passed to ELM or any cloud AI.

## Confidential contract terms

Emails that quote prices or terms marked "strictly confidential" or "not to be disclosed" are held, because confidential contracts may not be sent to public cloud AI tools.

## After approval

Once a compliance manager approves the shipment, the technical specification is no longer treated as confidential by default, and the booking, including its details, follows the normal flow in ELM.
