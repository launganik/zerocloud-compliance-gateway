# Decisions and escalation

> Demo knowledge base: illustrative content for the hackathon, not official Forto policy.

## Decision options

A compliance manager can approve a held case, which releases it to ELM, or reject it, which keeps it out of ELM and starts a customer follow-up. Every decision is written to the audit log with the reviewer, the time and the control-list version.

## When to escalate

Escalate to Data Protection when a case contains personal data beyond normal business contacts, such as passport or ID numbers. Escalate to Legal when a customer disputes a rejection or an export licence question cannot be answered from the documents.

## Sanctions

Sanctions screening is handled in TMS and is not repeated by the compliance gateway.
